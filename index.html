Beyond the code

Honestly, I’m still figuring myself out.

I like building things, but I also like getting lost in new places, meeting people I never expected to meet, filming random moments, and saying yes to things that scare me a little. I’ve moved around a lot, and every place has left something with me.

I don’t really know what I’m supposed to become yet. And maybe that’s okay.

For now, I’m just trying to live a little, build a little, and see where it takes me.
